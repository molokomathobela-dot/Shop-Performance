# Shop Performance Case Study

## Objective

Analyse how an online shop performed from January 2024 to June 2026 and identify revenue patterns, product and customer value, operational performance and opportunities for improvement.

The analysis is designed for the **Head of Operations**, using clear metrics, visualisations and data-backed recommendations.

## What I Aim to Understand

### 1. Revenue & Demand Patterns

- How much revenue did the shop generate?
- How many orders were completed and paid?
- What is the average order value?
- How does revenue change month by month?
- Are there recurring seasonal peaks or softer trading periods?

### 2. Product & Category Performance

- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which products sell the most units?
- Are the revenue and volume leaders the same?
- Which categories contribute the greatest share of commercial value?

### 3. Customer & Geographic Value

- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
- How do New, Regular and VIP customers differ in purchasing value?
- Which cities generate the most revenue?
- Which products are being purchased across different customer segments?

### 4. Operational & Payment Performance

- What proportion of orders are cancelled or returned?
- What proportion of payment attempts fail?
- Do payment failure rates differ meaningfully by payment method?
- Where may operational friction be contributing to revenue leakage?

### 5. Discount Behaviour

- Do higher discounts result in larger orders?
- Do discounts increase quantity or primarily reduce realised revenue?
- Where should targeted promotions, bundles or cross-sell opportunities be considered?

## Key Business Findings

The analysis identified:

- Revenue was broadly stable across the two complete years, with recurring monthly patterns.
- November and July were the strongest average monthly periods, while February was the softer period.
- Electronics contributed 50.6% of total revenue.
- Headphones were the highest-revenue product, while Power Bank led unit volume.
- Office Chair and Tablet were also significant revenue contributors.
- Regular customers generated the largest share of revenue.
- Tehran was the largest revenue market.
- Higher discount levels did not materially increase average quantity and were associated with lower total revenue.
- Cancelled, returned and failed-payment orders represent areas for operational monitoring.

## Business Recommendations

The analysis translates the findings into three practical actions:

1. **Protect the core**  
   Protect Electronics and high-value products while using cross-sell and bundles to increase basket value without relying on broad discounts.

2. **Plan around demand**  
   Use the observed November and July peaks and February's lower average to plan inventory, promotions and operational capacity.

3. **Target customer value**  
   Prioritise retention and basket-building among Regular customers and key markets, while using targeted offers rather than blanket discounting.

## Data Preparation

The analysis included:

- Duplicate detection and removal
- Missing-value assessment
- City-name standardisation
- Data validation and relationship checks
- Joining Orders, Products, Customers and Payments
- Revenue calculation
- Monthly and product-level aggregation
- Customer segment and geographic analysis
- Payment and order-status analysis

Revenue analysis was based on **Completed + Paid orders**.

## Business Outcome

The analysis moves from:

**Understand → Identify → Act → Measure → Optimise**

The final output provides the Head of Operations with a decision-ready view of revenue performance, product and customer value, operational friction and targeted opportunities for improvement.
