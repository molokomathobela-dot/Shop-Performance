# Shop Performance Case Study

## Objective

Analyse how the online shop is performing from January 2024 to June 2026 and identify operational patterns, performance gaps and opportunities for improvement.

The analysis is designed for the **Head of Operations**, using simple metrics, clear visualisations and actionable recommendations.

## What I Aim to Understand

### 1. Customer & Demand Patterns
- Who are our customers?
- Which cities and customer generate the most activity?
- How frequently do customers order?
- What does a typical order look like?

### 2. Product Performance
- Which products are most purchased?
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which products contribute most to overall sales?
- Are high-volume products also high-value products?

### 3. Operational Performance
- How is order volume changing over time?
- Are there seasonal demand patterns?
- Where are cancellations and returns occurring?
- Are payment failures affecting completed orders?
- Which payment methods experience more failures?

### 4. Customer & Order Behaviour
- Does order size change by customer segment or location?
- Do larger discounts result in larger orders?
- Do larger discounts increase sales or reduce revenue?

## Business Outcome

Use the findings to identify:

**What is working → What is underperforming → What requires operational attention → What should Operations do next.**

The final output will provide three practical, data-backed recommendations for the Head of Operations.
